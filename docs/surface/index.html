El Niño 2026/2027 · Sea surface temperature

Sea surface temperature: the 2026/2027 El Niño compared with past events

El Niño (A periodic warming of the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean, with related changes in winds and rainfall, recurring every two to seven years.) begins with warm water spreading east along the equator. These figures show the sea surface across the tropical Pacific in 2026/2027, in 2023/2024 and in the very strong El Niños of 2015/2016, 1997/1998 and 1982/1983.

Updated October 21, 2026. Data: NOAA observations, reanalysis and official forecasts. Select a figure to view it at full resolution.
