Ocean heat below the surface: the warm water behind the 2026/2027 El Niño

Most of an El Niño (A periodic warming of the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean, with related changes in winds and rainfall, recurring every two to seven years.)'s heat is stored below the sea surface. These figures follow the warm water in the upper 200 metres of the equatorial Pacific, in 3D, by temperature layer and along the equator, using NOAA's ocean reanalysis (A best estimate of past ocean or weather conditions, made by combining observations with a numerical model.).

Updated October 30, 2026. Data: NOAA observations, reanalysis and official forecasts. Select a figure to view it at full resolution.
